# Lumey Energy — Case Study

> **Solar Energy E-Commerce for Nigeria's Leading Provider**
>
> 🔗 [Live Demo](https://www.lumeyenergy.com) · [GitHub](https://github.com/Lumey-Energy/lumey_energy_nextjs)

## Overview

**Lumey Energy** is a full-stack e-commerce and customer management platform for Nigeria's leading solar energy provider, specializing in solar generators, power stations, and energy storage systems.

## Tech Stack

- Next.js, PostgreSQL, Prisma

## Key Features

- Product catalog and inventory
- Customer dashboard
- Solar equipment e-commerce
