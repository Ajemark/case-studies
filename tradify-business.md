# Tradify Business — Case Study

## Overview

Business trading and analytics platform with AI-powered market insights. Dashboard for tracking trades, analyzing trends, and managing business portfolios.

**Category:** Web2 / Fintech  
**Role:** Solo Developer  
**Status:** Live  
**Repository:** [tradify-business](https://github.com/Ajemark/tradify-business) (private)

## Challenge

Businesses need integrated trading analytics with AI-powered insights for informed decision-making. Manual tracking and analysis of market trends is time-consuming and error-prone.

## Solution

Built a trading analytics platform with:

- **Trade Tracking**: Comprehensive trade history and performance monitoring
- **AI Insights**: Machine learning-powered market trend analysis
- **Portfolio Management**: Multi-asset portfolio tracking and rebalancing
- **Trend Analysis**: Real-time market trend identification

## Architecture

```
Next.js Frontend
    ↓
Trading API
    ↓
AI Analytics Engine
    ↓
Database
```
